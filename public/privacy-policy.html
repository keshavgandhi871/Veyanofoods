Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 25, 2026

1. Our Commitment

We collect only the minimum data needed to provide you with our roasted snacks. Our mission is to fuel India with healthy alternatives while respecting the digital privacy of our customers.

2. Information We Collect

We collect your Name and Email address (via Clerk authentication) to manage your account. We also collect your shipping address and phone number specifically for order delivery and tracking fulfillment.

3. Data Usage

We use your data to fulfill orders, generate invoices, and send automated tracking updates. We may use your contact info to notify you of new healthy product launches and trial packs under the VEYANO brand that align with your interests in clean snacking.

4. The No-Sale Guarantee

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. It remains strictly within VEYANO Foods. We only share essential delivery details with our trusted logistics partners (Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC, Shiprocket) to ensure your parcels arrive promptly and safely.

5. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have full control over your information. You can request data correction, saved address removal, or account deletion at any time by reaching out to our support desk.
