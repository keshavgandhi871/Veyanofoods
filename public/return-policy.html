Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2024

1. Return Policy

As our products are perishable food items (Roasted Makhana), we generally do not accept returns once the product is delivered and accepted, for safety and hygiene reasons.

However, if you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery.

2. Refund Policy

Refunds will be processed in the following cases:

  • The product delivered was past its expiry date.
  • The packaging was damaged or tampered with before delivery.
  • The wrong item was delivered.

To claim a refund or replacement, please share a clear photograph or video of the issue at veyanofoods@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +91 9350598909.

3. Refund Processing

Once your claim is approved, refunds will be initiated to your original method of payment (for prepaid orders) or to your bank account (for COD orders) within 5-7 business days.

4. Cancellations

You can cancel your order within 12 hours of placing it, provided it has not already been dispatched. Once dispatched, cancellations are not permitted.
