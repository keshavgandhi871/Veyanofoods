Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 25, 2026

1. Our Commitment

We collect only the minimum data needed to provide you with our roasted snacks. Our mission is to fuel India with healthy alternatives while respecting the digital privacy of our customers.

2. Information We Collect

We collect your Name and Email address (via Google Sign-In) to manage your account. We also collect your shipping address and phone number specifically for order delivery and fulfillment purposes.

3. Data Usage

We use your data to fulfill orders and send tracking updates. Future Growth: We may use your contact info to update you on new healthy product launches under the VEYANO brand that align with your interests in clean snacking.

4. The No-Sale Guarantee

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. It remains strictly within VEYANO Foods. We only share essential delivery details with our trusted shipping partners (like Delhivery, BlueDart, or Shiprocket) to ensure your snacks reach you safely.

5. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have full control over your information. You can request data correction or permanent account deletion at any time by reaching out to our support team.
