Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

At VEYANO Foods, we take immense care in packaging our roasted snacks in airtight food-grade standing pouches to ensure they arrive at your doorstep in peak, shatter-crisp condition.

1. Pan-India Delivery Coverage

We deliver across India to almost all major pin codes through reliable national logistics partners (including Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC, and Shiprocket).

2. Shipping Charges & Free Delivery Threshold

  • Orders above ₹499: FREE Delivery across all serviceable Indian pin codes.
  • Orders below ₹499: A flat standard shipping fee of ₹50 applies at checkout.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): An additional courier handling fee of ₹79 applies for COD orders. Pay online via UPI or card to save this ₹79 fee.

3. Dispatch & Delivery Timelines

All orders are freshly packed and dispatched from our facility in Karnal, Haryana within 24 to 48 hours of order placement (excluding Sundays and national holidays).

  • Delhi NCR & Northern India: 2 – 4 business days
  • Metro Cities (Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Kolkata): 3 – 5 business days
  • Rest of India: 4 – 7 business days

4. Tracking Your Order

Once your order is handed over to our courier partner, you will receive an automated tracking link via email and WhatsApp with real-time updates.

5. Questions or Delivery Delays?

If you experience any unexpected delay with your parcel, reach out directly to us at veyanosupport@gmail.com or WhatsApp us at +91 9350598909 with your order number.
