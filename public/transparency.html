What's Inside VEYANO?

We believe you shouldn't need a degree in chemistry to read a food label. Here is our complete, transparent disclosure of ingredients, oils, nutrition, and safety standards.

🌿

1. Zero Palm Oil

We never use palm oil or hydrogenated fats. Our Classic Plain has 0% oil, and seasoned variants use under 1.5% rice bran oil mist strictly for spice adhesion.

🛡️

2. Zero Artificial Additives

No MSG, no artificial colors, and no synthetic preservatives. Seasoned with real kitchen spices and Himalayan pink salt.

🔥

3. 100% Hot-Air Roasted

Every seed is popped and roasted with controlled hot air, preventing oxidation and retaining natural micronutrients.

📜

4. Full FSSAI Compliance

Operating under FSSAI License No. 20826010000397 with strict batch quality grading in Karnal, Haryana.
